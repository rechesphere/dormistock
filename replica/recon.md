## Current Pain Points & Gaps in Original Site
1. WordPress/Elementor bloat: slow loading (>350KB DOM, heavy inline CSS, scripts).
2. Friction in Lead Generation: A static WPForms block at the bottom with 5 text fields and no guidance or diagnostic incentive.
3. Lack of Interactive Guidance: Buyers don't know what mattress or base fits their weight, firmness preference, or sleep habits.
4. Static Product Showcase: Generic cards with no firmness scales, size selectors, or quick quote drawers.
5. Incomplete Store Experience: Store addresses are buried in footer text without interactive tab switches or 1-click WhatsApp routing.

## Re-Engineering Goals (Hybrid Landing + Web)
- High-performance, zero-bloat standalone HTML5/CSS3/Vanilla JS.
- Interactive 3-Step "Virtual Sleep Advisor" (Wizard) converting visitors into high-intent qualified leads.
- Dual-channel conversion: structured WhatsApp message direct to closest store + automated form/webhook.
- Micro-interactions: Firmness gauge, size selectors with live price hints, slide-over quote drawer, interactive store locator.
- Agency-grade visual design: double-bezel cards, floating island navbar, electric cyan `#00bbff` + deep navy `#121240` palette, Poppins typography.
